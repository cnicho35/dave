# Instructor Guide

Use the baseline tidal-energy scenario to introduce learners to ROV-enabled marine-energy DOMD work without presenting simulation as an operational qualification.

## Suggested Session Flow

1. Confirm every learner can complete [Install and Verify](getting_started.md).
2. Demonstrate [First Marine Energy Simulation](first_simulation.md) and name the success indicators.
3. Pair learners so one pilots while the other records observations, then switch roles.
4. Assign [Challenge 1](challenges.md) and discuss how viewpoints limit inspection coverage.
5. Assign [Challenge 2](challenges.md) to practice an evidence trail.
6. Debrief the difference between a simulation observation and an engineering or safety conclusion.

## Observe These Capabilities

- Prepares, builds, and sources the workspace with appropriate support.
- Identifies Gazebo and RViz as different views of the same training scenario.
- States the inspection target and creates a stable observation viewpoint.
- Records evidence with enough context for a peer to reproduce it.
- Names a limitation of the scenario or an uncertainty in the observation.

## Reset And Support

Stop a session with `Ctrl+C` in the launch terminal, then relaunch the baseline scenario. Keep the baseline launch unchanged for early exercises. Use [Troubleshooting First Launch](troubleshooting.md) to guide recovery and document unresolved local setup issues for later support.

## Training Boundary

This material supports workforce development and familiarization. Field deployment, maintenance, decommissioning, and safety decisions require approved procedures, equipment-specific guidance, and appropriately qualified personnel.
