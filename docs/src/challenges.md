# Training Challenges

These exercises build from a working baseline. Complete [First Marine Energy Simulation](first_simulation.md) before beginning them.

## Challenge 1: Visual Inspection Of The TEC

**Objective:** Navigate the BlueROV2 to obtain useful visual coverage of the IKM3D tidal energy converter.

**Steps:**

1. Build and source the workspace.
2. Launch the tidal scenario with a PS4 controller or [Virtual Joystick](virtual_joystick.md).
3. Approach the TEC and obtain a view of the base, nacelle, and blades.
4. Record which surfaces were visible and which were obscured.

**Evidence:** Submit a screenshot or written inspection log that identifies the viewpoint and the visible component.

**Success criteria:** The evidence names the TEC component observed and distinguishes a simulated observation from a real-world equipment finding.

**Debrief:** Discuss how lighting, viewpoint, water conditions, and vehicle position affect the information available to an ROV operator.

## Challenge 2: Camera Evidence For Stakeholders

**Objective:** Find the simulated camera data and collect a reproducible piece of visual evidence.

**Steps:**

1. Start the baseline scenario and confirm the RViz camera display works.
2. Use the discovery commands in [Camera Evidence](camera_evidence.md) to identify the available image topic in your running system.
3. Capture a still image or use a locally tested recording tool approved for your environment.
4. Record the topic, timestamp, viewpoint, and TEC component in an evidence log.

**Evidence:** Submit the captured artifact and a short record explaining how it was obtained.

**Success criteria:** Another learner can use your recorded topic and steps to reproduce the camera view in the same scenario.

## Challenge 3: Inspection Plan

**Objective:** Plan a simulated inspection before piloting the ROV.

**Steps:**

1. Review [Understand the Simulation Scene](simulation_scene.md).
2. Define the component to inspect and the camera views needed to cover it.
3. Pilot the scenario and compare the plan with the path you actually used.
4. Identify one change that would make a repeat run clearer or more efficient.

**Evidence:** Submit a short plan, an annotated screenshot or log, and one lesson learned.

**Success criteria:** The plan states a target, a route or viewpoint strategy, evidence to collect, and a limitation of the simulation.
